[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
Red lanterns sway over a charcoal grill at a little yakitori shop.
Serve each sizzling skewer to your customer at the perfect moment.
Get it right and they say "Thank you!" — serve it raw or burnt and you'll hear about it. How much can you sell today?

■ FIVE KINDS OF SKEWERS
• Liver, negima (chicken and leek), thigh, tsukune (chicken meatballs) and wings
• Each one takes a different time to grill. Liver is quick, wings take their time
• When the meter above a skewer turns gold, it's ready

■ FLICK TO SERVE
• Flick a perfectly grilled skewer up to serve it
• Burnt one? Flick it down to toss it — no penalty
• Leave it on the grill and it turns to charcoal. Five misses and the shop closes
• The more you sell, the faster things grill
• Each finger grabs its own skewer, so you can serve with both thumbs at once

■ A NEW CUSTOMER EVERY TIME
• Office workers, grandpas, firefighters, even ninjas — a new customer for every skewer
• Their reaction tells you at once how well you grilled it

■ FESTIVAL FEEL
• Indigo noren curtains, swinging red lanterns, a glowing charcoal grill and rising smoke
• Flute-and-drum festival music that speeds up as your sales grow
• At closing time you get a receipt of today's sales and a rank, from Apprentice to Legendary Grill Master
• Your best score is saved — share your result and challenge your friends

■ ALSO
• Japanese and English
• No login needed, playable offline
• Free (contains ads)
